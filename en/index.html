02 / EQUIPMENT

Technology
behind the detail.

Our capabilities are based on the equipment shown below. Material and finishing choices are confirmed for each project.

01 / TEXTILESEPSON

SureColor
G6070

DTFilm printing up to 35.4 inches (about 90 cm) wide. Designs are printed on film and transferred to textiles through a suitable transfer process.

What we can make
  • Prints for T-shirts and hoodies
  • Sportswear and workwear
  • Tote bags and caps
  • Textile accessories
  • Small logos and large graphics
Materials

Cotton, polyester and blends, subject to the transfer process and a material test.

Technical details from Epson
02 / UV PRINTEPSON

SureColor
V1070

A4 UV flatbed printing directly on objects. White ink works on dark and transparent surfaces, while varnish adds gloss, matte and textured details.

What we can make
  • Keyrings and coasters
  • Pens and phone cases
  • Small signs and plaques
  • Photos on acrylic
  • Personalized gifts
Materials and size

Acrylic, wood, glass, metal, leather and selected plastics. Maximum media size 299 × 212 mm, height up to 70 mm and weight up to 3 kg with a jig.

Technical details from Epson
03 / LASER WORKRADEK

RK-6090-PRO

CO₂ laser cutting and engraving over a 600 × 900 mm working area. This machine does not print colour; it shapes, cuts and engraves.

What we can make
  • Acrylic letters and shapes
  • Wooden ornaments and signs
  • Engraved gifts
  • Prototypes and boxes
  • Signs and cut display pieces
Materials

Wood, MDF, plywood, acrylic, leather, card and rubber; mirrors, marble and coated metals may be engraved depending on the material. Cutting thickness depends on material and laser power.

Technical details from RADEK
04 / LARGE FORMATROLAND DG

TrueVIS
XG-640

Eco-solvent printing and contour cutting in one machine, with print and cut width up to 1,600 mm.

What we can make
  • Stickers and labels
  • Banners and posters
  • Window, wall and floor graphics
  • Signs and display panels
  • Vehicle graphics
Media

Suitable rolls of vinyl, films and banner material. Durability and use depend on the chosen media and finish.

Technical details from Roland DG
05 / PAPERCANON

imagePRESS
V1000

Professional colour digital printing for crisp text, photos and consistent colour. Supports sizes up to SRA3 and, with the right configuration, long sheets and media up to 400 g/m².

What we can make
  • Business cards and flyers
  • Brochures and catalogues
  • Menus and posters
  • Invitations and cards
  • Covers, envelopes and labels on suitable media
Media

Uncoated, coated, heavy, textured and synthetic papers, envelopes and other supported media. Size and weight depend on the installed trays and options.

Technical details from Canon

These capabilities describe what the equipment supports. We confirm each product, material, size and finish before production.
